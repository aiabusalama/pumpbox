Build reference · 138 V 1500 W DC submersible

Solar pump protection — wiring

Three GSE-HC455 panels in series produce 150.9 V open-circuit at 25 °C, and more when cold. The pump's controller card dies above 150 V. This circuit keeps the contactor open until the array has settled into a safe band.

  • 143 V DC array
  • 24 V control
  • 5 V logic
  • Data

1 · What each part looks like

The PZEM's USB cable ends in a screw terminal, so it wires to the 4-pin header directly. The RS485-to-USB conversion happens inside that white housing, which is why the cable has to reach a full-size USB-A port — and why an Arduino can't use it without a separate MAX485.

2 · Power path

Break DC only with the breaker. A DC arc has no zero crossing to extinguish it. Never separate a live 143 V connection by hand.

Before touching any terminal: open the breaker, then confirm 0 V with a meter. Panels produce voltage in daylight regardless of what the pump is doing.

3 · Control wiring

Set the LM2596 before it touches the Pi. These boards ship at anything from 1.2 V to 35 V. Feed it 24 V with the output disconnected, turn the trimmer until your meter reads 5.1 V, and only then wire it to pins 2 and 6.

The shunt's two fat bolts carry the full pump current and belong in the negative line. The two small screws are the sense tap and go to the PZEM. Swapping those roles destroys the meter.

5 · Before you close the breaker

  • Panels rewired to 3S1P — three in series, one string
  • Contactor coil is on 24 V, never on the 143 V bus
  • All three contactor poles chained in series, not paralleled
  • Shunt in the negative return; fat bolts carry current, small screws sense
  • LM2596 output measured at 5.1 V with the Pi disconnected
  • SPD is DC-rated — an AC unit looks identical and fails dangerously here
  • PZEM told it has the 50 A shunt: python3 test_hardware.py shunt
  • Every 143 V screw torqued, no stray strands bridging terminals
  • All grounds meet at one point — meter, buck converter, Pi
  • Meter reads 0 V across the pump terminals with the breaker open

Every failure mode lands on pump off: lose the Pi, the script, the 24 V supply or the meter link, and the coil de-energizes. The pump only runs while the controller is alive and actively holding the relay closed.
