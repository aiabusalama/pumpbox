REISBOT · Pump Protection Box

Wiring, three passes

Same backplate every time. Do one circuit, then the next — never all at once. Faded parts are not this pass. Terminals 1–5 are the box boundary: below them the wires leave through a gland.

PASS 1

143 V power — 8 wires

Breaker open. In at terminal 1, up the left, along the rail, through the contactor, back down to terminal 4. The return comes in at 3, through the shunt, to the − bus, out at 2.

PASS 2

24 V coil — 5 wires

The relay never touches 143 V. It switches the coil, and the coil switches the pump. Only the ⌒ hump is a crossing, not a joint.

PASS 3

Sensing and control — 6 wires

Thin wire only; none of this carries pump current. The dashed pair is millivolts — twist it and keep it away from the pump cable.

Sense screws, not the fat bolts The shunt's small screws go to the PZEM. The M6 bolts go in the pump cable with ring lugs. Reversed, 50 A passes through a millivolt input and the meter is destroyed.
Amps reading zero? The pump-side small screw is SH+. Reversed, the meter shows 0.00 A while volts look perfect — it imitates a dead pump. Harmless; swap the two.
All three poles, every time The contactor is AC-rated. One gap will not break 143 V DC — it welds shut, and then the pump cannot be switched off at all.

Water level relays — the two SSRC-04s

Two SSRC-04 units, hacked to run on 12 V DC. Each uses three rods: B deepest (common reference), L low, U high. Both powered from the new 12 V rail. The well is the hard safety layer; the tank is the automation.

C + NO output in series with the contactor coil (hardware dry-run block) — and a PC817 opto across it → Pi pin 40 C→GND pin 39, NO→Pi pin 38
Well = hardware safety, all modes C+NO physically in the contactor coil circuit. Dry well → coil can't energize → pump can't run, even if the Pi is dead. The PC817 opto just lets the Pi display it (pin 40), isolated from the 24 V coil.
Tank = automation, Auto only Straight to the Pi (pin 38). Full tank stops the pump in Auto; in Manual you may fill past it. The overflow float switch is the hardware backup here.
Screened probe cable Run the B/L/U rods on screened cable with the screen to GND, or cable capacitance reads as phantom water. Keep the EXC/common away from the probe cores.
