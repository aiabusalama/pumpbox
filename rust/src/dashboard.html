Probe placement

Each SSRC-04 uses three rods of different lengths from one mount. B is the longest — always in water, the common reference. Water "makes" a level when it rises to touch a rod's tip. The gap between L and U is the hysteresis that stops the relay chattering.

Well — dry protection
  • B deepest, above the pump — always submerged, reference
  • L minimum safe level — drop below and the pump STOPS (dry)
  • U recharge level — well must refill to here to restart

Reads WATER while the surface is above L. As the well draws down past L it opens (dry) and the pump waits until the water recovers to U. Keep B above the pump so the motor stays cooled.

Tank — fill control
  • B at the bottom — always submerged, reference
  • L restart level — pump resumes filling when it drops here
  • U full level, near the top — pump STOPS when reached

Reads FULL when the surface reaches U; the pump stops (Auto only). It empties to L before refilling, so it fills in slow cycles instead of switching on and off at the brim.

Dry running—

Both conditions must hold. Weak sun drops the current too — but it drops the voltage with it, which is what tells the two apart.

Timing—

Max hand run ends a run you started by hand after this long, so a pump left running by a closed phone does not pump all afternoon. It does not apply in AUTO — that mode exists to work an unattended day, and overvolts, a dry well or a dead meter each stop it on their own. 0 turns the limit off.

Meter errors allowed is how many silent readings in a row become a meter fault. Forgive strike after is how long the pump must run cleanly before one dry-run strike is dropped.

Threshold profiles

One action that rewrites every limit at once. Use this to put the real pump limits back after testing, rather than typing seven numbers and hoping.

Active profile: —

Set the profile before running the setup wizard. A profile rewrites i_max, dry_amps and dry_volts as well, so applying one afterwards discards the numbers the wizard measured from your actual pump.
