# Wire links — REISBOT level board

75 insulated wires, 3.6 m total.

These are the connections one side of copper could not carry. Each is
an insulated wire lying on the **component side**, with both ends
soldered from the copper side along with everything else. Fit them
**before** the parts — several run under IC bodies.

Print `2-wire-links-map.pdf` and tick them off.
